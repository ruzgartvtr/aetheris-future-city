Aetheris Written Description

Aetheris is a future city on the Aegean coast near Izmir. There is a school called Helix Academy in the north, and there is Vitalis Hospital next to the transit hub. In the centre, there is Canopy Park beside the Obsidian Library. People can walk, read, and rest there. By the harbor, there is Apex Arena, where people can play sports. On the ridge, there is Chronos Museum. There are electric buses and safe bike lanes in my city. My city has clean energy from the sun and the sea. Smart buildings can save power every day. People cannot breathe dirty air here, and they can live safely with clean streets. Aetheris is special because it is strong, green, and ready for real life.
